Why your own Assistant

You wouldn't rent your most valuable employee from a stranger's building. Your data shouldn't be any different.

A frontier cloud chatbot is powerful — but it works on your data only by first sending it out to someone else's servers. An Assistant built for you runs where your business runs.

01 · The honest comparison

Two ways to get an Assistant.

The frontier cloud chatbot
Powerful — but it works on your data only by sending it out first.
  • Your documents, CRM and email go to a third party's servers to be processed.
  • You're renting access to a model you don't own, on terms that can change.
  • Billing by "usage" — the more you use it, the more you pay, but you can't actually see how much more. Just usage.
  • A generic assistant pointed at your inbox — the same for every customer.
  • Compliance (GDPR, works councils, industry 4.0) is a problem for you to solve.
  • If they change a policy or raise a price, it lands on you.
Your own Assistant — Avellion
Runs where your business runs. You control the machine and the model.
  • Runs on a server inside your own building — your data never leaves your network.
  • Built on models and tooling you actually control — no single-vendor lock-in.
  • A fixed cost. No meter running in the background that you can't read.
  • Configured around your real workflows, tools and processes — not a generic bot.
  • Aligned with data-sovereignty requirements by design, not by afterthought.
  • It's yours. It keeps working the way you set it up, on your terms.
02 · What "on your hardware" actually buys you

Control you can't get from an API.

Data sovereignty

Your most sensitive information — contracts, customer data, financials — is processed where you hold the keys. Nothing is transmitted to a frontier provider.

A cost you can predict

No meter that runs up as you scale. A fixed cost means it gets cheaper the more your team uses it — and you'll never wonder what "usage" actually billed you.

Portable, not locked in

You're not tied to one company's model or roadmap. When a better model appears, we can point the same Assistant at it.

Configurable to you

Point it at your systems, your documents, your rules. It behaves the way your business does — because you set it up that way.

No usage cliff

Chatbots throttle or bill you harder as you grow. Your Assistant works the same on day ten and day one thousand — and it works 24/7/365 for you.

EU-aligned

Designed for the data-sovereignty culture of the EU — GDPR, works councils, and the regulated sectors that can't send data to a cloud API.

A frontier chatbot is a talented outsourced assistant. Avellion is a colleague who works in your building, knows your business, and stays.
