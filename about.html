About

A small, senior team that builds for you — not at scale. For you.

Tavellion d.o.o. builds Avellion — AI assistants that run on your own hardware, with your data staying yours. We're a compact, founder-led team: the people you talk to are the people doing the work.

01 · What we believe

On-prem. Your data. Your call.

We build on what we think the next decade of enterprise AI should be: models that run where your business runs, and software where you own the outcome.

On-prem first

The Assistant runs on a server inside your building — no third-party cloud in the path of your data.

Your data, yours

Documents, CRM and email are processed on your network. Full data sovereignty, EU-aligned by design.

Built around you

We configure for your workflows, not sell a generic chatbot. Start small, prove it, expand.
